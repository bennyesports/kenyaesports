Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Kenya Esports respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act of Kenya.

2. Information We Collect

When you register or use our platform, we may collect:

  • Your gamer handle/username and email address.
  • Your M-Pesa phone number for wallet deposits and withdrawals.
  • Match screenshots and gameplay verification records.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to manage tournament brackets, authenticate your profile, process wallet transactions safely, and communicate match updates.

4. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We do not sell or share your data with unauthorized third parties.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held on our platform.
